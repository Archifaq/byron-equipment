Boom lifts are aerial work platforms designed for elevated access where crews need both height and outreach. They are commonly rented for facade work, steel erection support, utility tasks, exterior maintenance, and projects where the platform must reach over landscaping, equipment, or building features. Articulating booms are useful around obstacles, while telescopic booms suit straighter high-reach work. For commercial renters, the main value is matching reach envelope, terrain capability, and platform capacity to the actual work zone rather than over-specifying a larger machine.

## Common Use Cases

- Exterior building maintenance, glazing, painting, and sealant work.
- Utility, lighting, and communications installation at height.
- Industrial plant access over pipe racks, machinery, or containment areas.
- Steel, framing, and cladding support where outreach is required.
- Tree-adjacent, site perimeter, and uneven-ground access tasks.

## What to Consider Before Renting

- Compare platform height, horizontal outreach, and up-and-over clearance.
- Choose articulating, telescopic, electric, hybrid, or diesel based on work path and environment.
- Confirm platform capacity for workers, tools, and materials.
- Review terrain, slope, turning radius, and stabilizing requirements.
- Plan transport access because larger booms need more room for delivery and positioning.

## Typical Specifications Range

| Equipment class | Common range | Typical fit |
| --- | --- | --- |

## FAQ

### When should I rent an articulating boom instead of a telescopic boom?

Use an articulating boom when the platform must move up and over obstacles. A telescopic boom is usually better for direct reach across open space.

### Can boom lifts drive while elevated?

Some models can reposition within limits, but rules vary by equipment type, surface, and site policy. Confirm operating limits with the supplier.

### What site details affect boom lift delivery?

Gate width, overhead obstructions, ground firmness, slope, staging area, and delivery truck access can all affect the rental plan.

### Should I request a larger boom than the measured height?

It is sensible to allow a margin, but oversizing can add access and cost challenges. Provide the work height and obstacle details for matching.
